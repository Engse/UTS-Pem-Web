RedBull :
- RB18 (2023) :
The RB18 was a complete redesign, with a Red Bull Racing Power Train under the cover, 18-inch tyres replacing the 13-inch rubber that had been used for more than 50 years, a completely different-looking rear wing, and a new-look front wing.
But it was the underbody and diffuser that was to make the biggest difference. It was designed to reduce the dirty air pushed out by the cars so it would encourage closer racing.
Max Verstappen and Sergio Pérez dominated the season, taking 17 wins between them and a total of 28 podiums, including five one-two finishes.
Max took his second title, and the team claimed their first Constructors' Championship since 2013.

- RB19 (2024) :
The RB19 has proven to be the best Formula One car ever built. Powered by Honda once again, the RB19 was unstoppable in the hands of Max Verstappen and Sergio Pérez.
In a 22-race season, the Bulls achieved 21 wins, a total of 30 podiums, and 15 pole positions.
The RB19 broke multiple records, including the most points in a season, the most consecutive wins by a manufacturer, and Max broke Sebastian Vettel’s record of nine wins in a row, by making it ten. The season ended with the team scoring 860 points, finishing 451 points ahead of Mercedes in second place.

W-14 (2023) : Forged from the lessons learned in a challenging 2022, the W14 is a stunning addition. The basic concept of the W13 has been retained, and development has focused on core performance areas. This results in a car that retains the distinctive DNA of its predecessor while introducing a combination of key changes visible at first glance – such as the gulley bodywork on the engine cover – and others beneath the surface. The car's striking appearance extends beyond its architecture. The iconic black livery from 2020 and 2021 has been refined as part of a significant project to reduce overall weight. Driving the W14 are Lewis Hamilton and George Russell, who will join forces for their second season together, supported by Mick Schumacher in his third driver role.
